# Privacy Policy for JARG - Just A Racing Game
 
**Last updated:** November 21, 2026
 
Penzkofer & Wittig GbR ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile game **JARG - Just A Racing Game** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, and how it is used.
 
## 1. Summary
 
Just Another Racing Game is designed to work fully offline. We do not require account creation, and we do not collect, store, or share any personal data from you through the App itself.
 
## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
 
We do not collect:
- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Location data
- Photos, contacts, or device identifiers
- User-generated content or in-game chat logs
- Any personal data for advertising or tracking purposes
The App contains **no ads** and does not integrate third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
 
## 3. Local Multiplayer (Bluetooth)
 
The App offers local multiplayer functionality using your device's Bluetooth connection. This connection is used solely to communicate directly between nearby devices during gameplay (for example, sharing race progress with a friend's device). No data transmitted over this local Bluetooth connection is sent to us or to any third party, and none of it is stored beyond the duration of the multiplayer session.
 
## 4. In-App Purchases
 
The App currently does not offer optional in-app purchases.

## 5. Analytics and Crash Reporting
  
If the App uses analytics or crash-reporting tools (e.g. to understand performance or fix bugs), only anonymized, non-identifying technical data is collected, such as device type, OS version, and crash logs. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
 
## 6. Children's Privacy
 
The App is rated for ages 12+ and may be used by children. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children or any other users, consistent with the practices described above. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.
 
## 7. Data Storage and Security
 
Any game progress, settings, or preferences are stored locally on your device only. We do not operate servers that store player data, and nothing is transmitted to us during normal use of the App.
 
## 8. Your Rights (EU/EEA Users – GDPR)
 
Since we do not collect personal data through the App, most data-subject rights under the GDPR (access, correction, deletion, etc.) do not apply, as there is no personal data of yours in our possession to act upon. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly and rights will be described here.
 
## 9. Changes to This Policy
 
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features that involve data collection (such as cloud saves or online leaderboards). Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
